Antik Türkiye — ambient sound in the 3D models
==============================================

The recordings in this folder are edited (trimmed, joined, levelled, converted
to MP3) from the sources below. scripts/build_ambience.py in the project
records exactly which file each clip came from and what was done to it.
Everything else heard in the models (wind, cicadas, sparks, chariot wheels,
the scops owl, doves, gulls and larks, the salpinx, the gourd rattle, the
reverb) is synthesised in the browser.
