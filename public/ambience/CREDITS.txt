Antik Türkiye — ambient sound in the 3D models
==============================================

Every sound here is either in the public domain (CC0) or synthesised for this
app. scripts/build_ambience.py in the project records exactly which file each
clip came from and what was done to it, or how it was synthesised. Wind,
cicadas, sparks, chariot wheels, the scops owl, doves, gulls and larks, the
salpinx, the gourd rattle and the reverb are synthesised in the browser.
